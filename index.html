Got Questions?

Frequently Asked
Questions

Have questions about how we work, project timelines, or custom scopes? Here are the most common questions answered.

Have a Question? Have a Question?

What do you need from me to get started?

−

Just share your project goals, any references you like, your brand guidelines, and a brief description of what you need, whether it's a SaaS platform, landing page, or dashboard. Screenshots or existing content are always helpful but not required.

Will the design work on mobile and tablet too?

+

Yes. Every design I deliver is fully responsive, optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile. Your users will get a seamless experience across all devices.

Can you follow my existing brand or start fresh?

+

Both. I can work within your existing brand guidelines to maintain consistency, or create a completely new design system from scratch, modern, clean, and tailored to your business.

How many revisions do I get?

+

Revisions are included until the design fully matches your vision and the agreed scope. I don't rush the process, the goal is that you're 100% happy with the final result.

How long does a project typically take?

+

It depends on the scope. A landing page is usually ready in 3–5 days. A full SaaS product or dashboard takes 2–4 weeks. I'll give you a clear timeline before we start so there are no surprises.

What does it cost?

+

Pricing depends on project complexity and scope. Projects start from $300. Send me a message with your project details and I'll get back to you with a custom quote within 24 hours, no obligation.

Still have a question?

+

Feel free to reach out directly, I usually respond within a few hours. You can message me on LinkedIn or send an email. I'm happy to jump on a quick call if needed.
